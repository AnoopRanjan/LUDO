Standard Ludo Rules & Guide

1

Deploying Tokens (Need a Six)

All tokens start in your yard base. You must roll a 6 to bring a token out onto your colored starting square.

2

Bonus Turn on Rolling 6

Rolling a 6 grants you another roll! However, rolling three 6s in a row cancels the third roll and passes the turn.

3

Moving Along the Track

Move your token clockwise along the 52-cell track according to the exact number shown on the dice.

4

Capturing Opponents (Knockout)

Landing on an opponent’s token on any regular square captures it! The captured piece returns to its base, and you earn a free bonus roll!

5

Safe Squares (Star Squares ★)

The 4 colored starting squares and 4 star-marked squares (★) are safe zones. Tokens on safe squares cannot be captured and can coexist safely!

6

Home Stretch & Winning

Tokens enter their own colored home column towards the central triangle. A token requires the exact roll to enter Home. The first player to bring all 4 tokens home wins!
